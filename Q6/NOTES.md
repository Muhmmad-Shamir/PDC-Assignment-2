When I ran the inventory benchmark, both maps used 10 workers to complete 50,000 operations, consisting of 25,000 reads and 25,000 stock updates. Both produced the expected final stock of 125,000 and displayed “Stock check passed: true,” confirming that no stock increments were lost. The synchronized map took 37.549 ms, with a throughput of 1,331,589.84 operations per second. ConcurrentHashMap took 14.876 ms, with a throughput of 3,361,095.99 operations per second. ConcurrentHashMap was approximately 2.52 times faster in this run. The combined read totals differed slightly because workers could read stock at different stages of the updates. These timings include thread startup and may change between runs.
